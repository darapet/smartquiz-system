Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Important: This page is general information, not legal advice, and may not cover every legal requirement that applies to you. Have qualified counsel review it before relying on it.

This Privacy Policy describes how Darapet Smart Learning System (“DaraPet,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), operating from Nigeria, handles information when you use SmartQuiz. SmartQuiz is available to users in multiple countries. If you have a privacy question or request, contact dev@darapet.name.ng.

1. Information we handle

  • Account and profile information: email address, name, age, education level, and any optional profile details you choose to add.
  • Study activity and content: quizzes you create or take, questions and answers, scores, learning history, notes, flashcards, study materials, group content, and feedback you submit.
  • Prompts and uploads: text prompts, extracted document text, filenames, and images or other files you choose to upload for quiz, AI, library, document, audio, or image features.
  • Device and browser data: basic technical information needed by the app and its providers to deliver requests, protect accounts, and troubleshoot service issues. SmartQuiz also uses browser storage such as local storage for session or feature state; clearing it may affect saved local activity or sign-in.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; provide quizzes, study tools, AI features, uploads, and group functions; save and restore your activity; send verification or service emails; respond to questions and feedback; prevent abuse; and meet legal obligations. We do not use your uploads for a purpose unrelated to the feature you requested except as permitted by law or with your direction.

3. Service providers and sharing

SmartQuiz uses external services to operate. Depending on the feature and fallback path, information may be sent to:

  • Firebase / Google: authentication, database, and app data storage.
  • Brevo: account verification, security, and other service emails; optional product emails may be managed through the app’s email preferences.
  • Groq, Mistral, Google Gemini, and Pollinations.ai: AI-generated study help, document processing, text-to-speech, or other AI features. Relevant prompts, extracted text, or files you submit may be sent for processing.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI: image generation or related AI features when enabled.
  • Cloudinary: library or user-provided media upload and delivery features.
  • tmpfiles.org and file.io: the image-editing flow may upload an image to a temporary file-hosting provider before processing. The app requests a one-hour expiry from file.io, but provider availability and retention are controlled by that provider; no fixed deletion period is promised here.

These providers may process information in countries other than your own and may have their own retention and privacy practices. Avoid uploading sensitive, confidential, or personal information unless you are comfortable sharing it with the relevant service and have permission to do so. We may also disclose information if required by law or necessary to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

4. Storage and retention

Account and activity information is stored with the service providers used by SmartQuiz and may also be stored locally in your browser for certain features. We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet legal requirements. Retention may differ by feature and provider; this policy does not promise a specific deletion schedule for every copy, backup, or provider log.

5. Your choices and requests

You can review or update some profile information and browser-stored data through SmartQuiz or your browser settings. To request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with your account, or to ask a privacy question, email dev@darapet.name.ng. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where required or permitted by law or where a provider has not yet removed a backup or log.

You can manage optional product emails through the email-preferences link included in those messages. Account and security messages may still be sent when needed to operate or secure your account.

6. Security and international access

We and our service providers use safeguards intended to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Protect your account details and do not submit passwords, payment details, or other highly sensitive information in study prompts or uploaded files. Because SmartQuiz is available in multiple countries and uses cloud and AI providers, information may be processed outside the country where you live, subject to applicable law.

7. Children and young users

SmartQuiz includes learning tools that may be used by younger students. If you are a minor where you live, use the service with parent or guardian involvement and obtain any consent required by local law. Parents or guardians may contact us about a young user’s information or request its review or removal, subject to applicable law.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this Policy when the service or its providers change. The current version and effective date will be posted here. For privacy questions or requests, contact dev@darapet.name.ng.
